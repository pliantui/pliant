This page covers the changes that aren't tied to one component: asking for a change in plain words, sharing and undoing changes, and display preferences. Every change is kept for the person who made it, and comes back on their next visit. [What users can do](/docs/what-users-can-do) lists the changes for each kind of component.

## Asking in plain words

<Callout
  variant="warning"
  title="Not fully implemented yet"
  data-unfinished="not-implemented"
>
  The Customize menu isn't in Pliant yet; today, users open the ask panel from
  its **Ask** button. [The ask panel](/docs/ai) can't yet add calculated
  columns, highlights, groupings, charts or defaults.
</Callout>

With the [ask panel](/docs/ai), a user can:

1. Open the [Customize menu](/docs/concepts/customize-menu) on the part of the page they want to change, and choose **Ask for a change…**. There are several ways to open it:
   - right-click the part;
   - click the **Customize** button in the part's header, or in the page header for the whole page;
   - pick from the **Customize** group in the <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd> menu, or press <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>.</Kbd> to ask about the part that has focus (<Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> on Windows and Linux).
2. Type what they want, or pick a suggestion.
3. See the change as a preview, then **Lock in** to keep it or **Discard** to drop it.

Nothing is saved until they lock it in.

Asking is most useful for changes you wouldn't build ahead of time, because each user wants something different:

- On a table: "add a column with each order's share of total value", "highlight overdue orders in red", "group by region and total the value" or "show value by region as a bar chart".
- On a row of cards: "add a bar chart of order value by region under the cards".
- On a form or a sidebar: "always set priority to high for me" or "open on tickets when I sign in".

## Share and manage changes

<Callout
  variant="warning"
  title="Not fully implemented yet"
  data-unfinished="not-implemented"
>
  This section shows how these features will work. Parts of them aren't in
  Pliant yet.
</Callout>

Users can:

- Save the current arrangement under a name, such as "Month-end review", and switch between saved views.
- Undo a change, or go back to any earlier point from **History** in the Customize menu.
- Send a colleague a link that opens the page arranged their way. The colleague sees a preview first and decides whether to keep it.
- Publish an arrangement as the default for their team. Each member can still adjust it.
- See which parts of a page they've changed, and reset any one of them with **Reset to default**.

## Display preferences

<Callout
  variant="warning"
  title="Not fully implemented yet"
  data-unfinished="not-implemented"
>
  This section shows how these features will work. Parts of them aren't in
  Pliant yet.
</Callout>

Users can:

- Switch a page between comfortable and compact spacing.
- Choose a larger text size, or reduced motion, for the whole app.
- Pick one of the themes your app offers, such as high contrast.
- Read Pliant's own labels in their language, laid out right to left for languages such as Arabic. See [RTL and languages](/docs/languages).
