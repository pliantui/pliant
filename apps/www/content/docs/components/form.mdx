## Usage

Render `PliantForm` inside your `<form>` in place of `FieldGroup`, with each field by its name. Pass your schema, so required fields can't be hidden.

## What users can ask

Users open the form's [Customize menu](/docs/concepts/customize-menu), from the **Customize** button in its header or by right-clicking it, and ask for the way they fill it in:

| Ask                                           | What changes                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| default priority to High for me               | Priority starts at High on every new ticket this user opens.               |
| fold the order number and CC into More fields | Order number and CC move into a **More fields** section the user can open. |

The preview above offers both asks. They play hand-written answers, marked scripted.

## Rules and guardrails

- A field your schema requires can't be hidden or folded away. Mark it `.optional()` or give it a `.default()` to let users hide it.
- Hiding or moving a field never remounts it, so it keeps its value and its validation.
- Users can't add fields, change labels you didn't allow, or change what a field accepts.

## Options

| Prop     | Type                        | Description                                                                                  |
| -------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `id`     | `string`                    | The form's node id: kebab-case, unique within the surface.                                   |
| `fields` | `Record<string, ReactNode>` | Each field by its name in your form library. The key order is the base order.                |
| `schema` | `PliantFormSchema`          | The form's schema, such as your Zod object. Fields it requires can't be hidden or collapsed. |

It also takes the props of a `div`, except `children`.

## Accessibility

- When submitting finds an invalid field inside More fields, the section opens and focus moves to the first invalid field.
- Fields keep shadcn's `Field` markup, so labels, descriptions and errors stay linked to their inputs.
