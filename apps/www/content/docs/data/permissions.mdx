title: Permissions and row security

Every dataset says which rows each user may load, which fields they may read, and whether they may use it at all. Pliant applies these rules on your server, to every query, total and load, so data a user mustn't see never reaches their browser.

With this dataset:

- Support staff can't use it at all.
- Sales staff see only orders from their own org and region, and never the margin.
- Admins see every order.
- No total covers fewer than five orders.

## Rows: `scope`

`scope` is required. It returns the conditions every row a user loads must match, such as `{ orgId: user.orgId }`. Each key names a field, or a column your table has, and must equal the value, or one of a list of values.

- `"public"` lets every user load every row.
- Returning `allRows` lets one user, such as an admin, load every row. It's a special value only Pliant exports, so a value read from your database or a request can never widen the scope by accident.
- Anything else, such as `undefined` or `{}`, throws. A missing scope fails closed.

`scope` runs synchronously, so look up what it needs, such as the user's region, before you load rows.

## The whole dataset: `access`

`access` on the dataset decides whether a user may use it at all. Only `true` allows. A user without access gets nothing: `authorizeDataset` returns `null`, and your route answers 403.

## Fields: `access`

`access` on a field decides whether a user may read it. A user who may not read a field gets no trace of it: it's left out of every load, and out of what the browser and the AI are told. A sort or filter on it fails as if the field didn't exist.

A row key field can't set `access`, because every row needs its key.

## Small groups: `privacy`

`privacy.minGroupSize` is the fewest rows a total may summarize. Totals over smaller groups are suppressed, so nobody can work out a single person's salary from the average of a group of one.

## Applying the rules

`authorizeDataset(dataset, user)` resolves all of it for one user, and returns a **grant**: the row conditions, the fields they may read and the smallest group size. `compileQuery` applies the grant to every query it builds. If you load rows another way, apply every part of the grant yourself. See [Custom data sources](/docs/data/custom-sources).

<Callout variant="warning" title="Hiding isn't a permission">
  Hiding a column in the table is a display preference. The data is still sent
  to the browser. To keep a field from a user, use `access` here.
</Callout>
