Each example is a small app with Pliant wired in. Open one, rearrange it, sort and filter its tables, or point at a part and ask for a change. Your changes are saved in your browser, so they're still there when you come back.

## Live examples

| Example                                    | What to try                                                                                                                      | Source                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Orders](/examples/orders)                 | Reorder the metric cards, sort, group and filter the orders table, add a calculated column, highlight rows, and ask for a change | [orders](<https://github.com/pliantui/pliant/tree/main/apps/www/app/(examples)/examples/orders>)                 |
| [Tickets](/examples/tickets)               | Reorder and hide form fields, fold the rarely used ones into "More fields", and set your own defaults                            | [tickets](<https://github.com/pliantui/pliant/tree/main/apps/www/app/(examples)/examples/tickets>)               |
| [CRM](/examples/crm)                       | Flag the deals with no recent activity, total each stage, and lay the stages out in two columns                                  | [crm](<https://github.com/pliantui/pliant/tree/main/apps/www/app/(examples)/examples/crm>)                       |
| [Settings](/examples/settings)             | Reorder and hide notification settings, and see your team's defaults                                                             | [settings](<https://github.com/pliantui/pliant/tree/main/apps/www/app/(examples)/examples/settings>)             |
| [Team workspace](/examples/team-workspace) | See the sidebar as you, your team or an admin: the team's default, your changes on top, and the admin's lock                     | [team-workspace](<https://github.com/pliantui/pliant/tree/main/apps/www/app/(examples)/examples/team-workspace>) |
| [Analytics](/examples/analytics)           | Rearrange the key metrics, reshape the chart, and sort and filter the latest orders                                              | [analytics](<https://github.com/pliantui/pliant/tree/main/apps/www/app/(examples)/examples/analytics>)           |

The asks in the examples replay recorded answers or play hand-written ones, so they respond without calling a model.

## Use an example in your app

The examples use the same registry components you install with the shadcn CLI. To start from one, follow [Your first customizable table](/docs/first-customizable-table) for the orders table, or [Forms](/docs/forms) for the ticket form.
