---
title: Make a dashboard customizable
description: Let each user arrange a dashboard's metric cards and charts their way, by reordering them, hiding what they don't need and choosing the layout.
---

On an orders dashboard, a finance lead might want the overdue count first and the regional chart gone, while a sales manager wants revenue by month at the top. This guide puts the dashboard's metric cards and charts in two regions, so each person can arrange them their own way.

Each `PliantRegion` holds a group of cards. The order of the `slots` keys is the default order, and the `className` sets your default layout. Users can now make these changes by dragging a card or from its menu, or by asking in the ask panel:

- reorder the cards, or pin one to the start;
- hide cards they don't need;
- switch a region between a stack, a row and a grid of up to four columns, from the `md` breakpoint up.

Give regions their spacing with `gap-*` classes. `space-*` and `divide-*` don't reach the cards.

## Let users ask

Add the ask panel and point mode, with a target for each region. See [How asking works](/docs/ai). Good suggestions for a dashboard:

- "put overdue first"
- "show the cards in two columns"
- "hide the top customers chart"

## Keep important cards visible

A card users must always see, such as a compliance notice, shouldn't sit in a region: render it outside, so no change can hide it.

## Charts that users can change

<Callout
  variant="warning"
  title="Not fully implemented yet"
  data-unfinished="not-implemented"
>
  This section shows how customizable charts will work. They aren't in Pliant
  yet. Today, a chart is a card in a region: users can move and hide it, but not
  change what it shows.
</Callout>

A Pliant chart is a view of a dataset, like a table. Users can ask to change it:

- "show this as a bar chart" switches the chart's kind;
- "group revenue by region" changes what it groups by;
- "only this quarter" adds a filter.

Each change is saved like any other, and the **Reset** button brings back your chart.
