title: Make a dashboard customizable
description: Let each user arrange a dashboard's metric cards and charts their way: reorder them, hide what they don't need and choose the layout.
---

A dashboard is where users most want things their way: the metrics they watch first at the top, and the charts they never look at gone. This guide makes a dashboard's cards and charts customizable with two regions.

</include>

Each `PliantRegion` holds a group of cards. The order of the `slots` keys is the default order, and the `className` sets your default layout. Users can now, through the ask panel:

- reorder the cards, or pin one to the start;
- hide cards they don't need;
- switch a region between a stack, a row and a grid of up to four columns, from the `md` breakpoint up.

Give regions their spacing with `gap-*` classes. `space-*` and `divide-*` don't reach the cards.

## Let users ask

Add the ask panel and point mode, with a target for each region. See [How asking works](/docs/ai). Good suggestions for a dashboard:

- "put overdue first"
- "show the cards in two columns"
- "hide the top customers chart"

## Keep important cards visible

A card users must always see, such as a compliance notice, shouldn't sit in a region: render it outside, so no change can hide it.

## Charts that users can change

  but not change what it shows.

A Pliant chart is a view of a dataset, like a table. Users can ask to change it:

- "show this as a bar chart" switches the chart's kind;
- "group revenue by region" changes what it groups by;
- "only this quarter" adds a filter.

Each change is saved like any other, and the **Reset** button brings back your chart.
