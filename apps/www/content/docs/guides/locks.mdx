---
title: Locks for admins
description: Let admins lock parts of a page, such as a required column, so team members can't hide or move them.
---

A lock keeps part of a page as an admin set it. Locking the orders table's **Status** column means no one on the team can hide or move it, even in their own view.

## Lock a part of the page

An admin opens the part's [Customize menu](/docs/concepts/customize-menu), from its header or by right-clicking it, and chooses **Lock for my team** or **Lock for everyone**. Locked parts show a small lock icon. Members can still use them; they just can't change them.

## What a lock covers

| Locked             | Members can't             |
| ------------------ | ------------------------- |
| A column           | Hide, move or resize it   |
| A card in a region | Hide or move it           |
| A whole region     | Change anything inside it |
| A table's sort     | Sort it another way       |

Your own code can lock a region too, with the `locked` prop. Users can't customize anything inside it:

```tsx
<PliantRegion id="compliance" locked slots={{ notice: <ComplianceNotice /> }} />
```

## Locks aren't security

A lock is a policy for the page, not a way to keep data from users. The page runs in the user's browser, and they control it. Anything a user mustn't see belongs in your [dataset permissions](/docs/data/permissions).
