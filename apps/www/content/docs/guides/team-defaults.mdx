In this guide, you let a team lead arrange the orders page for their team, and an admin for the whole organization. Every member starts from that default, and can still adjust it for themselves.

<Steps>

<Step>Store changes on your server</Step>

Team defaults need server storage. Set up the [Drizzle store](/docs/storage/drizzle) or another [self-hosted store](/docs/storage/self-hosted).

<Step>Tell the route who's on which team</Step>

Return the user's `org` and `teams` from `getUser`, and decide who may publish with `canPublish`:

<include cwd lang="ts" meta='title="app/api/pliant/storage/route.ts"'>
  snippets/storage/drizzle-route.ts#route
</include>

<Step>Rank teams</Step>

If users can be on several teams, pass `teamOrder` to `PliantRoot`, highest precedence first, so it's clear whose default wins.

<Step>Publish from the page</Step>

A team lead arranges the page, then chooses **Publish for my team** from the table toolbar or the ask panel. They see a preview of what their teammates will get, and confirm. Admins also see **Publish for everyone**.

</Steps>

## What members see

- The team's default applies the next time they load the page.
- Their own changes still win on anything they changed themselves.
- **Reset** brings them back to the team's default, not your original page.

## Changing or removing a default

Publishing again replaces the team's default. **Remove team default** takes it away, and members go back to the org's default or your page.
