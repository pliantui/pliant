Pliant is in early development.

Pliant lets the people who use your shadcn app reshape it to fit their work. They can reorder, hide and resize the parts of a page, and sort, filter and group its tables, within the rules you set. Their changes are kept for their next visit.

To see it, open the [Orders example](/examples/orders) and press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> <Kbd>.</Kbd>, or <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>.</Kbd> on a Mac. Pliant outlines the parts of the page you can change.
