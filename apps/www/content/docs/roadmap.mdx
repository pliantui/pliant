title: Roadmap
description: What's planned for Pliant, and what isn't built yet.
---

Pliant is pre-1.0. These lists describe intent, not commitments, and carry no dates.

## Planned before 1.0

- Published `@pliantui/*` packages on npm and a hosted component registry.
- A `pliantui init` command that sets Pliant up in your app.
- Guides for Vite, React Router and the Next.js Pages Router.
- Team and org overlays: publishing a layout as a team default, with a self-hosted server storage adapter.
- Conflict-safe saves in the storage adapter, for two tabs or devices saving at once.
- Saved views: named arrangements a user can switch between.
- Manual editing of regions without AI.
- Deriving the ask panel's `context` from your regions.

## After 1.0

- Packs of saved views, with signing.
- Approximate percentiles.
- Relations between datasets and a query layer.
- Vue and Svelte.
- Export to A2UI.
- A history panel.
- Several answer variants to choose from in the ask panel.
- Storage adapters for Prisma, MySQL and SQLite.
- A per-user theme choice.
