title: Guardrails and limits
description: A security summary of what users can't change on a page, the size limits on every change, and why none of it is access control.
---

Guardrails stop users, team defaults and AI answers from breaking a page by mistake: hiding a status column users rely on, or moving **Delete** next to **Save**. Limits keep every change small. Both are useful, and neither is a security boundary. [Guardrails](/docs/concepts/guardrails) explains them for everyday use, and [Guardrail rules](/docs/reference/guardrails) gives the exact behaviour.

## What guardrails enforce

- Parts flagged `critical`, and required form fields, stay visible.
- An action flagged `destructive` never moves closer to the `primary` action.
- A prop value that carries meaning, such as a `destructive` variant, can only be set by your code.
- Shortcuts can't take keys the browser, the operating system or a screen reader needs.
- Form field defaults must pass the field's schema, and fields stay inside their form.
- Users can hide what you built, but never delete it, and clearing their changes always brings back your page.

A change that breaks a rule is skipped, and the rest of the change applies.

## Limits

Every change has fixed bounds: at most 500 operations, labels of at most 80 characters, expressions of at most 128 nodes, column widths from 16 to 2,000 pixels, and so on. A change past a limit is rejected. Server-mode queries have their own caps, such as a maximum page size. [Limits](/docs/reference/limits) lists them all.

## Why guardrails aren't access control

Guardrails run in the user's browser, on code the user controls. A determined user can change what runs there, remove a guardrail, or read anything your server sent. Locks for team and organization defaults are the same: a policy that keeps a page consistent, not a barrier.

So treat guardrails as protection against mistakes, and keep every real restriction on your server:

| You want to                      | Use                                                         |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Keep a field from a user         | Field `access` in the [dataset](/docs/security/permissions) |
| Keep rows from a user            | The dataset's `scope`                                       |
| Stop a user saving team defaults | `canPublish` in the storage route                           |
| Keep a notice on screen          | A `critical` flag, or render it outside Pliant's regions    |
| Stop accidental deletes          | `destructive` and `primary` flags                           |
